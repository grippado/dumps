# Recipe: landing (think like a product marketer)

Use to present a project, a product or an idea.

1. **Hero.** A headline that states the benefit, one supporting sentence, one primary call to
   action and one secondary.
2. **Proof.** A screenshot, a live demo link, numbers or a quote.
3. **Features.** 3 to 6 cards, each a benefit headline and one sentence.
4. **How it works.** 3 numbered steps.
5. **Call to action again** at the bottom, plus links (docs, source, contact).
