# Recipe: story (think like a magazine editor)

Use to tell how something happened over time: a project, an incident, a launch, a journey.

1. **Hero.** A title with a point of view (not "Project report"), a one-line dek, and 3 or 4
   numbers that frame the story (days, people, commits, cost).
2. **Cast.** Who or what took part, as small cards (a person, a tool, a machine), each with
   one line on its role.
3. **Timeline.** Vertical, newest last. Each event: real date and time, a short verb label
   ("decided", "shipped", "broke"), one or two sentences, and who acted. Mark day changes
   and long gaps. Never estimate times: use the ones from the source or omit them.
4. **Turning points.** Pull 2 or 3 moments out of the timeline as larger callouts with the
   lesson learned.
5. **Where we are now.** What is done, what is next, as two short lists.

Tone: concrete and human, past tense for events, present tense for the current state.
